# Origins of Rome

In ~800 BC, Latins were pastoral herdsmen and farmers descended from Balkan migrants, with little developed art, religion, or writing. Etruscans were skilled craftsmen with extensive trade networks, including routes through Rome. Rome's location was strategic due to its easy Tiber crossing, defensive hills, and position on trade routes. Its founding story is largely mythical because early records were lost, but its broader picture of Latin–Etruscan conflict and a Latin sphere south of the Tiber broadly fits archaeology. The legend was constructed later using common mythological elements and influences from other cultures.

Soldiers equipped themselves; the richest provided horses. A 100-man Senate advised the king and became the basis of the later patrician class.
