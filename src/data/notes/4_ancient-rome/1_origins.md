# Origins of Rome

In ~800 BC, Latins were pastoral herdsmen and farmers descended from Balkan migrants, with little developed art, religion, or writing. Etruscans were skilled craftsmen with extensive trade networks, including through Rome. Rome's location was strategic due to its easy Tiber crossing, defensive hills, and position on trade routes. Its founding story is largely mythical because early records were lost, but its picture of Latin–Etruscan conflict and a Latin sphere south of the Tiber broadly fits archaeology. The legend was constructed later using common mythological elements and influences from other cultures.

Soldiers equipped themselves; the richest provided horses. A 100-man Senate advised the king and became the basis of the later patrician class.

## Timeline

### Legends of foundation

- ~12th century BCE: Aeneas arrived in Latium, inherited the kingdom, and established the Tiber River as the boundary with the Etruscans.
- ~12th–8th century BCE: Aeneas' son Ascanius founded Alba Longa; generations later, Numitor was exiled by Amulius, while his daughter Rhea Silvia gave birth to Romulus and Remus, who were abandoned by the river and adopted by a she-wolf.
- ~8th century BCE: Twins discovered their ancestry, killed Amulius, restored Numitor to the throne at Alba Longa and founded Rome at the place they were abandoned (on Palatine Hill); Remus was killed in the dispute.
- 753 BCE: Romulus traditionally founded Rome and became its first king.

### Romulus's institutions

- ~8th century BCE: Romulus established a Senate of 100, an army of ~3,000 infantry and 300 cavalry, and seized women from neighboring communities, including Sabines who went to war. Conflict ended in peace and union under two kings, three tribes, and a Senate expanded by 100 Sabines.
- ~8th century BCE: Tribes were divided into the Comitia Curiata: a popular assembly associated with royal authority and religious matters.
- ~8th century BCE: The Sabine king died, leaving Romulus as sole ruler; he fought neighboring peoples for ~40 years and died in Rome.
