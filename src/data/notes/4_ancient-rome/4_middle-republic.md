# Middle Roman republic

Rome emerged as the dominant power in central Italy, while the political integration of plebeians (consulship + first plebeian censor and dictator) strengthened the Republic. Rome increasingly became involved in conflicts among neighboring peoples and began fighting longer wars against stronger opponents, setting it on the path toward domination of Italy.

## Timeline

- 343–341 BCE: Rome fought the First Samnite War after becoming involved in the conflict between Campania and the Samnites, semi-nomads from central Apennines (Samnium) who came down from the hills in 300s BC.
- 342 BCE: Plebeian reforms required at least one of the two consuls each year to be a plebeian and ten-year period before a person could hold the same office again.
- ~341 BCE: The First Samnite War ended with Rome having gained the upper hand but without decisively defeating the Samnites.
- ~341 BCE: A mutiny by Roman soldiers in Campania was peacefully resolved by Marcus Valerius Corvus, who secured amnesty for the soldiers.
- 340 BCE: The Latin War began after Latin League demanded full Roman citizenship and one of the consuls be from Latin communities.
- 340 BCE: Rome and the Latins fought at the foot of Mount Vesuvius in a closely matched battle, Rome victorious.
- ~340–338 BCE: Rome defeats Latin communities individually (Samnites assisting them), subduing Latium, abolishing Latin league, and granting different degrees of citizenship depending on their conduct.
- ~338 BCE: Roman colonies were established on land taken from communities that had resisted Rome.
- ~334–331 BCE: Alexander of Epirus campaigned in Magna Graecia at Tarentum’s request, fighting the Lucanians and Bruttians and temporarily strengthening the Greek cities of southern Italy.
