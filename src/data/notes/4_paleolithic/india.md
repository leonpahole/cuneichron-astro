# India

## Timeline

- ~300,000–10,000: Hand axes and other stone tools are widely used across the subcontinent.
- ~300,000–10,000: Hunter-gatherer communities occupy river valleys, caves and rock shelters.
- ~300,000–100,000 BCE: Stone-tool traditions spread across the subcontinent.
- ~250,000–150,000 BCE: “Narmada Man”, a hominin fossil (don’t know species) discovered in the Narmada Valley, provides evidence of archaic humans in the Indian subcontinent.
- ~100,000–65,000 BCE: Homo sapiens reach the Indian subcontinent and gradually spread across it.
- ~30,000–10,000 BCE: Rock art develops at sites including Bhimbetka, depicting animals, hunting and human activities.
