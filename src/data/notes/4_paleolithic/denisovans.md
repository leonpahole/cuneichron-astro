# Asia - Denisovans

Discovered in 2010 in Denisova Cave in Siberia. Sister group to Neanderthals.

## Timeline

- ~50,000 BCE: _Homo sapiens_ interbreed with Denisovans in Asia. Present-day populations in Melanesia, Papua New Guinea, and many Aboriginal Australians carry the highest proportion of Denisovan DNA (up to about 5%), while some East Asian populations also carry smaller amounts.
- ~40,000 BCE: Denisovans disappear from the fossil record (exact timing uncertain).
