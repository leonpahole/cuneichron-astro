# Global - Homo Sapiens

## Timeline

- ~70,000–60,000 BCE: Major migration out of Africa begins.
- ~65,000 BCE: Humans reach Australia.
- ~40,000 BCE: _Homo sapiens_ becomes the only surviving human species.
- ~40,000–20,000 BCE: Domestication of dog (from common wolf ancestor) - humanity's earliest domesticated animal.
- ~16,000–14,000 BCE: Humans enter the Americas via Beringia and/or the Pacific coast.
