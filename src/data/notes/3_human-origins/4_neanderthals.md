# Neanderthals

Sister species, larger average brain volumes but different organization, short, muscular, adapted to cold, likely possessed language abilities, cared for their injured, buried their dead.

Named after Neander valley (Dusseldorf, Germany) where they were first discovered.

## Timeline

- ~700,000-600,000 BCE: _Homo heidelbergensis_ evolves from earlier _Homo erectus_-like populations.
- ~430,000 BCE: European populations of _Homo heidelbergensis_ begin evolving into Neanderthals.
- ~400,000 BCE: Earliest definitive evidence of humans (Neanderthals) intentionally _creating_ fire (discovery at Barnham in Suffolk - tools and iron pyrite used to strike sparks).
- ~300,000 BCE: Those populations are distinct enough to be classified as _Homo neanderthalensis_.
- ~250,000 BCE: Neanderthals routinely use fire, wooden spears, and sophisticated stone tools (Mousterian industry).
- ~120,000 BCE: Intentional burials appear.
- ~60,000 BCE: Birch tar adhesive is produced.
- ~50,000 BCE: Neanderthals interbreed with modern humans. Nearly all people of non-African ancestry today carry about 1–2% Neanderthal DNA.
- ~40,000 BCE: Neanderthals disappear.
