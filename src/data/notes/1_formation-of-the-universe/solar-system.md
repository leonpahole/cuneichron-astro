# Solar System - Formation

## Timeline

- ~4.6 billion BCE: A cloud of gas and dust collapsed under gravity, forming the Sun at its centre.
- ~4.6 billion BCE: A rotating protoplanetary disk formed around the young Sun; dust grains collided and accumulated into larger bodies.
- ~4.6 billion BCE: Planetesimals formed through the accretion of dust and rock, eventually becoming the planets.
- ~4.5 billion BCE: The Sun entered its main-sequence phase, beginning the stable hydrogen-fusion stage that continues today.
